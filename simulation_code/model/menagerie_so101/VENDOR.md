## Patches (`so101_bench.xml` vs `upstream/so101.xml`)

1. `base` quat `0.7071068 0 0 0.7071068`: the legacy model's base yaw. The SO-101's joint conventions are then the legacy
   ones, and the repo's `q_so100 = SIGN * q_so101 + OFFSET` (`contracts/coordinates.py`) holds exactly. Gripper FK
   matches the legacy model to 9e-9 m.
2. Tip pads renamed to the code's names: `fixed_jaw_box4..7` -> `fixed_jaw_pad_1..4`, `moving_jaw_box2/3` ->
   `moving_jaw_pad_1/2`. Collision geometry unchanged. The SO-101 has no moving-jaw pads 3/4, so the grasp detector's
   closing axis uses pad 1 on this arm (`simulation/arm_profiles.py`).
3. Sites `fixed_jaw_tip` / `moving_jaw_tip` and the body `wrist_camera_mount` (the repo's mount mesh, visual only,
   massless, and camera `wrist_camera`) are placed at the SAME physical pose as on the SO-100 bench model. The SO-100's
   declared poses go through the rigid transform between the two models' gripper (and moving-jaw) frames at matching
   joint angles; across 200 random poses that transform varies by 0.54 mm and 0.001 deg (the two CAD models differ
   slightly). At the reset and survey poses the camera lands within 0.2 mm and 0.001 deg, the tips within 0.23 mm.
   The closure metric, the real-arm jaw-height guard and the hand-eye calibration keep their meaning. Under identical
   fit settings the hand-eye residual is 115.55 px on the SO-100 and 115.59 px on the SO-101.
4. Menagerie's own `camera_mount` body (camera `wrist_cam`, PCB, two collision boxes) removed. Its camera sits about
   55 mm from the calibrated one, and its boxes would count as unsafe cube contact.
5. Part materials mapped onto the bench's `white` (printed parts; the scene builder makes it near-black PLA) and
   `black` (servos), which the appearance randomization resolves by name.
6. `meshdir="../assets"`; marker site `arm_profile_so101_menagerie` (group 5, invisible) identifies the arm to the code.

Physics (option, defaults, actuators, collision classes) is untouched here. `tools/prepare_bench_scene.py --arm so101
--dynamics bench|menagerie` builds the scenes `bench_pick_replace_v1_so101` (the bench's kp 50 / dampratio 1 /
armature 0.1 / frictionloss 0.1 / forcerange 3.5, 0.002 s Euler) and `bench_pick_replace_v1_so101_menagerie`
(Menagerie's kp 998.22 / kv 2.731 / forcerange 2.94 / damping 0.6 / armature 0.028, 0.005 s implicitfast). Both keep
Menagerie's gripper collision classes (condim 6, priority 1). The joint map is `measured_20260908b_so101`
(`tools/derive_joint_map_so101.py`: the measured SO-100 map in this model's conventions, exact).

## Grasp geometry (checked 2026-11-06)

- The convex hulls sit behind the jaw surfaces (the August failure was hulls filling the mouth).
- A 20 mm cube fits at 60-70 mm depth.
- The tip pads close parallel within about 2 deg at a 16-20 mm gap.
- Deeper than about 84 mm the moving side has only a mesh hull, so grasp at the tip pads.
- The teacher (pad 1, unchanged settings) certifies 30/30 on both scenes with about 152 strict-grasp frames per
  episode.
